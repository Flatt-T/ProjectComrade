## What has actually run

With seed 14077, each player deployed its MCV at tick 1. Using valid build-menu, queue and placement commands, the two power plants were placed at ticks 177 and 353. The Allied tent and Soviet barracks were placed at 587 and 821. Cross-side barracks eligibility was rejected. The Allied refinery was placed at 1,985 and a harvester spawned. Rifle infantry exited at 2,044. Cancelling a partially funded war factory restored the paid amount at 2,047. The first observed deposit raised harvested credits to 50 at 2,589. Queuing was deliberately serial in this fixture. These are observations of the currently audited COMRADE rules. Run the same Node script against the compiled package on an isolated checkout. Verify both event arrays, balances and checksums before comparing a 3D client.

The bridge transaction oracle ran five fixture groups, covering one damage quote across multiple spans, two engineers competing, command retry, save/resume, insufficient credits, invalid access, stale revision, hostile scaffold damage, partial repair, obstruction timeout and exactly-once restoration. A mixed quote with one missing span and three damage on a survivor is 601 credits, with the ceiling applied once to the combined surviving deficit. Admitted at tick 20, the route stays closed at 469 and reopens at 470 if clear. This is an executable expectation for an authored system. Runtime navigation, picking, shields, cargo and real devices have separate proofs. [Oracle](bridge_oracle.py) · [Observed assertions and terminal states](bridge-oracle-results.json)

The 30 FPS phone baseline proposes p95 ≤33.3 ms, p99 ≤50 ms and p95 local touch feedback ≤150 ms. The desktop target proposes p95 ≤16.7 ms and p99 ≤33.3 ms. A median, screenshot, headless render or average FPS cannot decide this gate. Reference timings for every historical turn, clip and projectile need matched execution; keep them null until measured. The [original 3D fixture](../art/field-service-tractor.gltf) exercises motion and picking separately from the measured simulation loop.
