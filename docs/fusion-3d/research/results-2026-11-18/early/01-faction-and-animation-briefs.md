## Tiberian Sun and Firestorm

The Nod mobile repair vehicle REPAIR uses RepairBullet. The GDI transport ORCATRAN retains owner and cost but has TechLevel -1. Create a treaded deployment scene and a legged deployment scene. Start each moving with an active order. Stop, align, play the transition, reserve the final footprint, issue an attack, undo deployment, and navigate off the tile. Visually distinguish a Nod artillery carriage and a GDI walking platform through body movement, stance and barrel framing. ## Red Alert 2 and Yuri's Revenge

In the pinned rules the GI E1 can occupy buildings, costs 200 and uses a shared GISequence in the art comparator. Allied FV is an IFV with one passenger and Gunner=yes. The Allied Grizzly MTNK is 700/300 cost/strength and Soviet Rhino HTNK is 900/400. Both miners cost 1,400 and have strength 1,000. Soviet HARV has a rapid-fire weapon and Allied CMIN lists none. Separate tests must cover their return journeys. Allied identity in the nine-country game includes British sniper, German tank destroyer, French Grand Cannon, Korean Black Eagle under the internal house Alliance, and American Airforce Command HQ. Soviet house-specific examples include Russian Tesla Tank, Iraqi Desolator under Arabs, Cuban unit under Confederation, and Libyan truck under Africans. Country gating also carries disabled candidates such as HOWI. Inspect technology and scenario eligibility before pre-populating a multiplayer roster. The expansion manual identifies the Magnetron on PDF page 21, the siege chopper on page 18 and slave economy on pages 19, 22–23. For SMIN show deploying, worker emergence, individual harvest/return, depletion and packing up. China's war factory produces an Overlord at 2,000 with build time 25 and 1,100 health. Its default command set and three turret upgrade alternatives must retain their own state. The GLA Technical costs 500 with build time 5 and a supply stash costs 1,500 with build time 15. The publisher manual describes air, truck and worker collection plus tunnel access across PDF pages 29, 35, 39 and 45. Build one map with the same supply source and three faction-specific depot approaches. Capture flight approach and descent, wheel stop and unload, and the walkers' gather/return cadence. Then block the ground route, destroy the receiving building and restore a new depot. Count credits only at the authoritative deposit. ## Review before release

Compare a declared capability with three observations where relevant: a valid player menu action, a forbidden player action and a scenario-authored instance. Record the rules profile, source anchor, replay timestamps, simulation result and presentation capture for each. Retail-hash gaps for RA2/YR and community art timing, Covert Operations runtime and Counterstrike retail scenario completeness, all-title mode availability, exact map-specific rules, platform variants, performance and end-to-end animation playback remain separate evidence tasks. A verified ZIP, page or source registration establishes a historical data point. It cannot demonstrate mobile framerate, replay determinism or complete campaign coverage.
