# How the battlefield should sound

The same click can select a unit, ask it to move, or issue a move that the server rejects. A player looking across a busy phone screen needs to hear which of those things actually happened. Five publisher XML trees provide concrete precedents. The [research data](command-and-ambient-audio.json.gz) contains 4,134 attributed declarations and relationships in 33 files. They include adviser events, faction-specific alternatives, exclusion relationships, music conditions and selected unit-bound sounds. Each row names the source file and line. A declaration count measures the selected records. A voiced line, distinct recording, sound duration or number of simultaneous channels needs its own evidence.

Bind the sound and dust to that contact. Keep continuous loops keyed to entity and state. On pause, seek, loss of visibility, death or interrupted deployment, remove or rehydrate the appropriate active loop exactly once. |
| Terrain and tactical state contribute to music | The Blue Zone music file describes a base-building track and a latched exploration condition. Tiberian Twilight enumerates six mixer snapshots including Default, Explore, Combat and Shell. | Commission original short stems that crossfade at phrase boundaries. Use a player-entitled exploration/base/combat state. Switch between camera areas while hidden enemies move. A music transition must never reveal those opponents. Compare with music muted and maintain equivalent tactical information. |
| Damage and repair remain legible at the bridge | The original Fusion bridge has independently pickable deck and water surfaces in its four visual states. The reconstruction oracle records exactly when the route reopens. | Distinguish impact, immediate order denial, scaffold activity, completion and route availability. Emit the completion cue from the authoritative transition once. Underwater or scenic effects may change with listener position. A suppressed or muted clip must never alter the path graph or the result. |

The authored voice direction should distinguish operators by job, faction, level of urgency and channel treatment. Record fresh performances of original lines. Keep acknowledgements short enough to clear a rapid succession of commands, vary repetitions without changing their meaning, and place speech in the centre while spatializing movement around the command camera. Give speech, effects, ambient sound and music independent levels. Subtitles need a speaker/source indicator, urgency and a stable display position without intercepting touch. Original materials and cue timing require a production listening pass.

## Playback acceptance

1. Run the seeded first battle with earphones and a single phone speaker. Enable captions and issue the same orders in silence. Record command, simulation event, eligible listener, active cue, suppression reason and UI text with synchronized timestamps.
2. Test one unit and 32 units receiving the same order. Interrupt deployment halfway, queue a denial, then resume play from a saved state. The unit never acknowledges an impossible completed transition. A reconnect cannot replay old alerts at full volume.
3. In the bridge scene run a ship below, a vehicle above and a walker approaching the deck. Capture frame tags, foot contact, terrain family and listener position. Compare a close camera with the ordinary tactical camera. The underpass, approach and local adviser have distinguishable spatial or channel behavior.
4. At the published 256-unit/64-building load, measure command-to-feedback, late cues, voice drops, device route changes, muted/resumed browser audio and the effect of cold loading on frame pacing. Record each device and the time series. A suite passing in software rendering establishes fixture behavior only.
