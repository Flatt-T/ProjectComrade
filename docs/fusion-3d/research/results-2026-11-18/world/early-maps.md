The TS rules mirror gives CABHUT immunity, the bridge repair flag, 2,000 strength and negative tech level. Its selectability and targetability are disabled. The RA2 comparison table enables selectability and legal targeting while keeping immunity and the repair flag. Inspect these independent axes before implementing HUD or picking. To derive original terrain from this evidence, select a snow map with a road, a temperate map with tubes and a hut-bearing river. For each make a fresh geometry design, record material classes beneath/above the crossing and stage one unit on each traversable surface. Verify bank access, on-deck closure, independent underpass and hut approach. A flat texture showing a hut can satisfy art identification only. The authority must open and close navigation, and a player must be able to distinguish inaccessible, damaged, under repair and traversable states at ordinary touch zoom.
