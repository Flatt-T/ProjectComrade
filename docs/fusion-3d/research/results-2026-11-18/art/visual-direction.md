# Fusion visual direction and motion findings

**Production intent.** At normal tactical zoom a player should identify a unit's role, side, direction and current action in the time it takes to choose an order. Up close, the same model should retain functional joints, materials and believable weight. Establish the look with a matched scene containing a base, small infantry, larger vehicles, open ground, cover, a shoreline and a crossing. Review it with normal HUD, the real movement rate and the final exposure. All proportions, palettes, clip timings and thresholds proposed below are original Fusion decisions for comparison and playtesting.

For the blind comparison, render the two tanks, two infantry-production structures and two naval-production structures at 32, 56 and 96 CSS pixels of projected maximum dimension. Use the same grey neutral light, same owner masks and identical background. Ask reviewers to select the family and tactical role. Then trade one object between owners in a live test. The two questions “who built it?” and “who owns it now?” must both remain answerable.
