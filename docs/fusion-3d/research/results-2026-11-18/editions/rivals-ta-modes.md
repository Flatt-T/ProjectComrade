# Rivals and Tiberium Alliances

## Rivals roster and timing

[The faction ledger](rivals-ta-rivals-reference.tsv) follows the GDI and Nod community navigation lists. It contains 96 side-specific rows: 32 combat units per side, nine commanders paired to nine powers, ten faction-building entries and four MCV/harvester entries. A shared name such as War factory has a separate row per faction. A late-game unit belongs to the tech facility group even when its movement is aerial. The approximately contemporaneous [GDI navigation](https://cnc.fandom.com/wiki/GDI_base_(Rivals)) and [Nod navigation](https://cnc.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Rivals_Nod_arsenal) are secondary indices. They establish the scope of this census. They cannot identify the executable patch in which each unit was first playable.

[The primary source register](rivals-ta-rivals-sources.json) contains 18 dated EA unit showcases and two EA-hosted update posts. Twenty unit rows carry an actual faction, production building, rarity, Tiberium cost, player unlock level, source date and a combat or presentation mechanic from a showcase. For the two earliest units the detailed stats come from the linked 1.0 notes. The additional-mentions column is a conservative text lookup against article bodies. A mention can describe a counter, an ally or lore. It alone establishes neither balance nor availability. Earlier and later tunings can conflict. For example, the Orca Bomber showcase gives a cost of 120. The August 2023 post signed by the Rivals team gives a change from 110 to 100. Preserve both dates. The Catalyst Gunship article labels its building Helipad while the Nod community roster calls its air building Air tower. This discrepancy is retained in the row instead of silently translating it. [EA Bomber](https://www.ea.com/able/news/new-unit-showcase-orca-bomber) · [August 2023 update](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/command-and-conquer-franchise-en/balance-patch-notes---latest-patch-august-2023/7276141) · [EA Gunship](https://www.ea.com/able/news/new-unit-showcase-catalyst-gunship)

EA describes live unit control in head-to-head matches on phones and tablets. The 1.0 notes document changing the opening income curve, so make the economic model part of the selected edition profile. The notes also describe shareable replays and saved decks. Preserve small-screen ownership, unit selection and actionable unit-level controls. The aerial scout squad, heavy minimum-range artillery and split-beam vehicle all read differently when rendered at the same default size. [EA launch announcement](https://news.ea.com/press-releases/press-releases-details/2018/EA-Announces-Command--Conquer-Rivals-Launches-Worldwide-December-4/default.aspx) · [1.0 notes](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/command-and-conquer-franchise-en/update-1-0-notes/7159073) · [EA Juggernaut and Giga-Cannon](https://www.ea.com/able/news/new-unit-showcase-meet-juggernaut-giga-cannon)

Three authored reference scenes translate these facts into repeatable work. Use original models, effects and sound in the Fusion implementation.

1. **Artillery setup and response.** Set up the GDI walker three hexes from the target. Enter the stationary state, establish sight and issue its attack. Check setup, aim, multi-hex impact, and recovery visually and in the command log. Move a counter inside its minimum range during the test. The aiming indication must distinguish an unengageable close target from an expired command. Use the six-tile coverage as the documented edition detail. Record its exact footprint from that edition before locking a shape.
2. **Contested path and silhouette.** Move a Battering Ram across rock and dispatch a ground counter. Confirm that traversal, turret/target classification, foot contacts and the final headbutt remain coherent at the production camera. When a normal ground unit receives the same order, the navigation preview must reflect its own traversal rules. The Ram is explicitly a four-legged ground unit able to climb rock. [EA Battering Ram](https://www.ea.com/able/news/new-unit-showcase-battering-ram)
3. **Gas and squad membership.** Lay a Chem Buggy cloud while moving, then trigger Jade's power over it. Run an independent pass with the Catalyst Gunship. Its own area effect has a different documented interaction with chemical units, existing enemy clouds and airborne targets. Log unit/team, cloud owner, affected tiles and resulting orders. In a separate Cyberwheel/Fanatic interaction, remove one squad member, redraw the formation and check the documented damage reduction or nearby ally effect. [EA Chem Buggy](https://www.ea.com/able/news/new-unit-showcase-chem-buggy-drone-swarm) · [EA Catalyst Gunship](https://www.ea.com/able/news/new-unit-showcase-catalyst-gunship) · [EA Cyberwheel](https://www.ea.com/able/news/new-unit-showcase-cyberwheel) · [EA Fanatic](https://www.ea.com/able/news/new-unit-showcase-fanatic)

Community pad-control explanations say that control of a majority of *pads* advances the shared missile charge and changes the displayed controlling faction. Count control per pad before aggregating it across pads. A hex inside a contested pad is a different state from ownership of the whole pad. Test contested hexes, equal pad counts, ownership changes just before launch, and simultaneous destruction. Record a named map, client version and replay for exact contest, stall, launch, direct-base victory and tie timing. [Community pad explanation](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/command-and-conquer-franchise-en/nuclear-missile-in-command--conquer-rivals/7247172)

## Tiberium Alliances factions and economy

[The 194-row layout catalog](rivals-ta-alliances-reference.tsv) records a community adapter’s exact identifier, placement context, faction or event variant and immutable source line. Both playable sides contribute 17 base mappings, 15 defense mappings and 14 offense mappings each. The Forgotten group has ten base and 17 defense mappings. Two infected faction variants contribute 17 base and 15 defense mappings each. Ten fortress-defense mappings and one event-base mapping complete the table. These are adapter entries. Each side's generic harvester and its crystal/tiberium placement variants account for three entries. Multiple contexts reuse symbols or names. The adapter author explicitly marks infected entries as requiring testing. [Community adapter at commit 13b64a2](https://github.com/zbluebugz/CnC-TA-Opt/blob/13b64a25ee8897ea988ecfbaf2c4a96a203e5f12/CnCTAOpt.link.user.js) · [Base-builder interface](https://www.cnctaopt.com/)

The adapter serializes a nine-column, twenty-row layout and uses different vertical offsets for defense and offense. On a Forgotten target, attacking formations come from the viewing player’s base. Green and blue resource fields have separate entries for bare fields and an occupied harvester position. Woods, scrub, oil and swamp have distinct resource-type cases. A layout symbol can mean different things by row context. This data establishes map authoring vocabulary and example coordinates. It supplies no terrain art, walkability rule or authoritative current-server path cost. [Layout serialization](https://github.com/zbluebugz/CnC-TA-Opt/blob/13b64a25ee8897ea988ecfbaf2c4a96a203e5f12/CnCTAOpt.link.user.js#L470) · [Terrain cases](https://github.com/zbluebugz/CnC-TA-Opt/blob/13b64a25ee8897ea988ecfbaf2c4a96a203e5f12/CnCTAOpt.link.user.js#L546)

EA documents five resources. Tiberium builds structures, crystals fund units, and advanced levels require power. Refineries generate credits. Credits and research are account-wide across bases. Crystal and Tiberium can move between bases for credits. Power is stored at its base. Research is obtained through combat rather than a production building. An adjacent silo supports a harvester, an accumulator supports a power plant, and a power plant supports a refinery. Up to two unclaimed packages accumulate per producing building. EA separately documents a 25% production-speed increase per adjacent crystal field for a plant or per nearby Tiberium field for a refinery, capped at four supporting fields. The field-based speed bonus and the claimable package require different counters and indicators. The nonplayer Forgotten control the central fortress and launch attacks in waves. A destroyed Forgotten base leaves a territorial ruin. Camps have their own removal conditions. [Publisher FAQ](https://www.ea.com/games/command-and-conquer/command-and-conquer-tiberium-alliances/faq)

1. **Base adjacency test.** Place the two harvesters on the correct field colors, add a silo beside one and claim two packages. Separate the resource balance, production rate and package count. Exercise one through five neighboring bonus fields and check the four-field cap. Record rotations, corner adjacency and adjacency eligibility from a reference world before configuring the precise neighbor mask.
2. **Stored power and transfers.** Build two bases in a test world with different balances. Transfer crystal subject to a credit debit. Check account-wide credits/research, local base power, and the origin/destination balance displays. Record the actual transfer fee from the target world before choosing an amount.
3. **Forgotten defense formation.** Fill an attack row with GDI or Nod offense, give the Forgotten defense a wall, ranged defense and mobile unit, and retain the defending base’s own structures. Verify that swapping the inspecting player changes attack-side origin while preserving the opponent's defense. Save a before/after occupancy trace and a territory result after destruction. The pinned adapter's own/world and event branches supply data-link evidence; unit speeds, arrival times, targeting and current world modifiers require a live or recorded build.

To reproduce the catalog, run `python3 rivals-ta-extract-alliances.py CnCTAOpt.link.user.js rivals-ta-alliances-reference.tsv` against the pinned adapter. The extracted identifiers are factual references. The input game code, server data, art and animation remain outside this package.
