# What the derivative editions contribute to Fusion

## Sole Survivor

Westwood’s English manual gives 23 entries in its unit quick reference, including six infantry, twelve vehicles, a Visceroid and four dinosaurs. The player chooses one unit on a channel. The play loop centres on position, pickups and the objective. In the documented capture-the-flag variant the flag returns to a home base. Football asks the carrier to reach the designated endzone. Infantry-only and dinosaurs-only channels restrict selection and use a timed kill result. The original manual also documents offline practice. The dated Windows readme identifies the 1997 Windows 95/DirectX release. [Manual, PDF leaves 4, 7–14](https://oldgamesdownload.com/wp-content/uploads/manuals/command-and-conquer-sole-survivor_win_manual_en_2vc.pdf) · [Westwood readme](https://oldgamesdownload.com/readme/command-conquer-sole-survivor-windows-readme-english/) · [Full named manual ledger](sole-survivor-manual-facts.json)

The five ordinary upgrade dimensions are armour, shot power, speed, reload and range. Radar, healing, shroud and special pickups change what the player sees or can do. The published 1.04 notes put the four default crate weights at 840 wooden, 100 green, 50 steel and 10 orange per thousand, with a default orange effect of 30 seconds. They make team scores cumulative even when a player leaves. Keep the server settings, 1.04 defaults and the later 1.05 patch distinct. A server chooses its configuration. [Westwood patch notes preserved by CNCNZ](https://cncnz.com/features/technical-support-help-guides/sole-survivor-v101-v105-patch-history/) · [1.05 patch catalog](https://cnc-comm.com/sole-survivor/downloads/patches/1.05)

Author an optional arena profile. Give a dropped flag its own entity and ownership ledger. Test pickup/return, disconnect while carrying, return to the correct scoring surface and duplicate scoring events. Snapshot individual timed modifiers and a stable RNG stream. Retain the civilian/dinosaur and projectile/structure classifications in the reference census. A roster in this manual does not establish each channel’s exact patch-specific availability.

## Dune lineage and the current Fusion pack

The Dune 2000 manual gives the three major houses distinct acquisition and combat roles. For example, the Missile Tank is manufactured by Atreides and Harkonnen. Ordos can order it through the Starport. The multiplayer Thumper belongs to any house in the manual and requires the upgraded Barracks. The existing Fusion pack limits its Thumper to Atreides and references the Barracks plus Outpost. That is a concrete rules difference. Preserve both profiles and use the relevant version in a Classic comparison. The existing pack’s deployment, upgrade and economy values are expressly approximate. [Manual pp. 40–45](https://oldgamesdownload.com/wp-content/uploads/manuals/dune-2000_win_manual_en_8jn.pdf) · [Audited Fusion pack](https://github.com/Flatt-T/ProjectComrade/blob/84f411478f01ed62365a2e4d1d316eb57d9e193c/packages/fusion/src/packs/dune.ts#L170)

The ledger indexes 39 unit/structure reference entries from pp. 36–45 plus the economy-role Harvester. It cross-references all 50 current Fusion units and structures by exact ID and line. Thirty-nine current definitions have a mapped manual role, including distinct Fusion variants of a role. Eleven lack a match in this reference selection. The manual also describes roles outside the unit pack, including the Frigate, Sandworm and a palace missile. Keep those as logistics, environmental or support rows as appropriate. A manual speed in km/h describes the printed unit and needs a separate movement measurement before conversion to simulation ticks. [Dune 2000 field ledger](dune2000-manual-to-fusion.json)

Dune II documents house-specific units and shared industrial construction. Emperor documents three primary houses and up to two selectable subhouses, including a projector that creates a controllable decoy and a Guild tank with a recharge-limited displacement. Treat these as references when designing acquisition and visual cues. Dune 2000 compatibility remains the programme’s named delivery profile. The other titles’ factions and art require separate provenance. [Page-indexed lineage](dune-lineage.json)
