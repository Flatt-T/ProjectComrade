## Procedure

1. Read the frozen profile manifest, all era reconciliation reports, capability/terrain matrices, adaptation decisions and generated task manifest. Confirm the reviewer is independent of the content being certified; assign separate reviewers where needed.
2. Reconcile the publisher released-title inventory and later-release ledger with every planned profile, including expansions, countries/generals/subfactions/classes, console additions, campaign/FPS/browser/mobile items and separate Dune compatibility. Verify cancelled/unused content never inflates shipped totals.
3. Check explicit faction denominators: RA2 nine countries plus Yuri expansion profile; Zero Hour three base plus nine specialist general profiles and separate Leang challenge treatment; Kane's Wrath nine variants; RA3 Empire; C&C4 six faction/class combinations. Check additions and exclusions in each tech tree.
4. Audit all high-risk mechanics and a documented sample of simpler records against immutable primary sources and shipped observation fixtures. Include bridges/repair huts, transformations, subterranean/tunnels, mind control, garrison/transport, crawler economy, naval/water production, superweapons and lifecycle edges.
5. Inspect provenance/license separation and current live-service source dates. Verify no borrowed art/audio/map files are assumed distributable from code licences, and no pre-registration announcement becomes a present release claim.
6. Run schema and task DAG validation. Choose several generated tasks as a cold reader: can an agent identify exact files, data/profile, capability contracts, required outputs, acceptance and next handoff without guessing? Reject ambiguous cards and require localized corrections.
7. Verify terrain completeness measures geometry/material/props/lighting/hazards/path/buildability, animation includes full required states, and art/rendering does not conceal missing gameplay. Check full-3D/native Classic separation and latest MMO boundary rules.
8. Publish findings with severity, affected entity/profile/capability/task, source evidence, precise remediation and required retest. Critical source/behavior errors block affected downstream tasks; minor editorial defects do not stop unrelated progress.
9. After corrections, issue a signed coverage certificate identifying exact manifest hashes and accepted scope, remaining unresolved exceptions and conditions. If any era remains evidence-blocked, certify only completed portions and display the gap prominently; never issue an unconditional every-unit-complete statement.

- Treat this card as a bounded contract. Read the named context and dependency outputs first. If required evidence is missing, record a precise blocked datum and continue only with independent work; never fill a field with an assumed zero, a plausible remembered value, or another era's value.
- Preserve native Classic fidelity independently from source-era research profiles and the newly authored Fusion rules. A Fusion change is an explicit adaptation decision with rationale and acceptance tests, never a retroactive claim about the historical game.
- Cite each nontrivial historical statement or numeric datum to an exact manual page, immutable source path/line or symbol, or timestamped observation in an identified shipped build. Search snippets, fan wikis, source declarations and official announcement text cannot alone prove shipped behavior.
