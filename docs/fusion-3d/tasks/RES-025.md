## Procedure

1. Pin the first-battle seed to verified RA1 Allied/Soviet reference profiles from RES-005 plus explicitly cited bridge observations. Enumerate exact source rows before accepting downstream values; this manifest is the bounded denominator, not an invented final roster. Required seed coverage: temperate ground, river banks, navigable water, approach ramps, separate deck/underpass surfaces, bridge destruction and TS/RA2 manual-supported repair-hut evidence, with historical versus Fusion-default differences explicit.
2. Define the schema for all era inventories and reconcile the pinned seed's actual map/theatre/asset inventory. Identify climates, biomes, ecological corruption, urban/industrial/military/civilian themes, architecture, surfaces, elevation, cliffs/ramps, shorelines/water depths, roads/rails, vegetation, neutral structures, hazards and lighting/weather. Keep discovered themes distinct from verified shipped ones.
3. Map each theme to cited missions/maps/assets rather than generic labels such as grass, snow and desert. Record scale/silhouette palette, original-asset needs and interaction features. Preserve snow/ice, urban interiors, lunar, mutated red-zone and naval geography where proven.
4. Create bridge source profiles per era and bridge type: deck segments, supports/ends, ramps, collision/surface layers, ground/naval passage below, buildability, targetability, armor/health, destruction propagation and repair access. Confirm real historical repair-hut rules in TS and RA2 against manuals and the pinned builds.
5. Treat RA1 source bridge repair under OBSOLETE as inactive evidence until a real supported profile proves otherwise. Active Destroy_Bridge_At is evidence for destruction, not universal reconstruction. For later 3D games inspect exact maps/scripts rather than copying TS/RA2 assumptions.
6. Observe occupied-span destruction: units on deck, beneath span, approaching or queued; aircraft; garrisons/transport; path reservations; reconnecting segments; damaged adjacent roads. Record result with timestamps and initial state. Mark sources where behavior cannot be observed.
7. Observe repair/reconstruction: eligible actor, approach point, interaction duration/cost, engineer consumption, partially damaged versus missing sections, ownership/hostile access, units/wrecks in restored space, path reopening, interruption and repeated damage. Build a conflict table for historical differences.
8. Observe other terrain mechanics where supported: thin ice/cracking, deformable ground, tunnels, pavement/buildability, Tiberium/veinholes, fire/toxin/radiation, cliffs, islands, water/naval travel, ore/supply regeneration and destructible civilian structures. Distinguish visuals from simulation.
9. Hand evidence to world/art and engine teams; author a new Fusion policy only through RES-027 with clear decisions for debris, falling units, water passage, repair ownership/cost and offline-world boundaries. A visually broken bridge without graph/physics consequences fails the research-to-implementation contract.
10. Produce an extension manifest for every remaining era/faction/capability/theatre/feel/adaptation row, with responsible source/PKG owner, required evidence and bounded follow-up work. Global reconciliation belongs to RES-028/029; no absent era is silently omitted or marked complete here.
