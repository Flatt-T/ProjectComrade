## Procedure

1. Pin the first-battle seed to verified RA1 Allied/Soviet reference profiles from RES-005 plus explicitly cited bridge observations. Enumerate exact source rows before accepting downstream values; this manifest is the bounded denominator, not an invented final roster. Required seed coverage: command response, economy/production rhythm, infantry and tank locomotion, turret/fire/reload cadence, naval/air interactions, scouting/fog, tactical audio, bridge interruption/rebuild and victory/defeat readability.
2. Select bounded benchmark scenes across eras and factions using completed catalogues: harvesting/base growth; early infantry skirmish; armor/artillery engagement; naval/amphibious assault; subterranean/teleport/stealth; garrison/capture; transformations; large superweapon; occupied bridge destruction/repair; crawler class play. Each scene must identify exact profile and entities.
3. For each scene document source setup, camera distance/angle, unit counts, terrain, resources, command sequence and observations. Quantify command responsiveness, movement/turning, attack wind-up/reload/projectile travel, damage feedback, death persistence, production/harvest cycles and tactical time-to-response.
4. Document faction-specific silhouette/readability at actual mobile zoom, animation anticipation/recoil/reload/deploy/repair/death, turret/barrel/track/foot/rotor behavior, weapon traces, sound categories, selection feedback and environmental destruction. Separate measurable timing from subjective art review.
5. Choose acceptance metrics and tolerances from source observation or explicitly authored Fusion goals. Do not invent universal numerical thresholds merely to fill a table. Require enough evidence to compare video captures or deterministic event traces.
6. Create a source-authenticity rubric and a separate unified-Fusion gameplay rubric. Assess whether unique capability and production restrictions change decisions; a unit that looks right but behaves generically fails identity review.
7. When the user video becomes available, connect verified timestamp observations from RES-002 to relevant scenes. If it remains blocked, leave that evidence source unavailable; official-source benchmarks remain usable.
8. Define mobile usability checks that retain tactical expressiveness: selection/attack/repair/capture/ability targeting, accidental-input prevention, silhouette/terrain contrast, sound priority and visible power/stealth/bridge states. Avoid monetisation or unrelated platform scope.
9. Deliver original benchmark scene specifications and review scorecards to QA/art/engine. Require independent assessors to record concrete failed observations and corrective tasks, not simply approve screenshots as polished.
10. Produce an extension manifest for every remaining era/faction/capability/theatre/feel/adaptation row, with responsible source/PKG owner, required evidence and bounded follow-up work. Global reconciliation belongs to RES-028/029; no absent era is silently omitted or marked complete here.
