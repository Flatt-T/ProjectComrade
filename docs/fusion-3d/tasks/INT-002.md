## Procedure

1. Read era mechanics/feel fixtures and all approved Fusion adaptation records.
2. Create controlled mirrored and cross-faction scenarios covering economy timing, harassment, scouting, expansion, infantry/armour/artillery/air/naval counters and recovery.
3. Compare command response, acceleration/turn/turret timings, engagement/reload rhythms, harvest routes, production emergence, power pressure and tactical audio/animation cues.
4. Run human play sessions where required by the visual/gameplay gates and AI scenario sweeps separately; record device, seeds, rules and participants without unsupported capacity claims.
5. Investigate systematic uncounterable interactions and inaccessible build paths; propose smallest profile-scoped balance changes with before/after results.
6. Accept changes only through the decision log and rerun affected matchups, preserving Classic and saved legacy profile values.
