## Procedure

1. Read the locked reference manifest and catalogue schema. Confirm an exact legally accessible source profile, source/config hash and observation environment before extracting facts. If the profile is unavailable, list precise blocked evidence and perform only source discovery/classification that can be supported.
2. As this is an era work package, first generate bounded research leaves: one faction availability/production graph, at most 25 simple raw-definition classifications per intake leaf, and one complex entity or at most five closely related simple entities per behavior-evidence leaf. Give each leaf the relevant source excerpt/locator, profile, output schema, acceptance and reviewer. A coordinator must not claim an entire era was completed in one context window.
3. Create an unfiltered candidate census from active source/config enumerations, manuals, mission references and map object lists. Preserve raw IDs; classify buildable units, buildings/defenses, walls/gates, neutral tech/civilian objects, heroes, campaign-only units, support powers, upgrades, deployed/transformed forms, summoned/spawned children, projectiles, wrecks, unused/cancelled records and visual-only helpers.
4. Extract full faction availability and production graphs, including prerequisites, negative restrictions, expansion inheritance, capture access, tech upgrades, per-mode legality and one-per-player restrictions. Record economy/build timing, mobility/target layers and every signature interaction with source evidence.
5. Locate the exact shipped INI/config baseline and reconcile it with the pinned official Generals source. Read active object behavior modules, weapon/armor/locomotor definitions and faction command sets, including civilians and campaign-only naval/script assets.
6. Document USA/China dozer construction and GLA worker construction, site progress/vulnerability, supply collectors and return cycle, power consequences, neutral oil/hospital/repair facilities and faction secondary-income structures.
7. Measure tunnel/garrison transport, faction transport ejection and occupancy, GLA holes/rebuild, salvage/scrap levels, horde/nationalism, propaganda/healing, fire spread/firestorm, toxins/radiation, stealth/detection, crushing and garrison-clear rules.
8. Record Generals experience/power unlock trees, command-center abilities, support aircraft paths, targeting and cooldowns. Preserve unique China/GLA/USA producer and upgrade relationships rather than merging all units under a single factory.
9. Document physical 3D collision, projectile travel/impact, suspension/turning, infantry animations, wreck/scorch persistence and original camera/readability; identify animation timing coupled to weapons and exits.
10. Classify campaign-only ships, trucks, trains, tech buildings and destructibles, including what can move/fire and what is scenery. Terrain inventory includes verified desert, temperate, snow and urban/industrial themes, roads, cliffs, water and authored destruction.
11. For each behavior-critical entity create a repeatable observation fixture with initial state, input sequence, measured outcome, units/tolerance and video or event evidence where available. Measure movement/turning, acquisition/firing/reload, health/armor/damage, costs/build times, special ability state and death/ownership transitions. Unknown values remain explicit blockers for that entity.
12. Document geometry, silhouette, material, faction cues, required animation states, effects, audio and destruction as observable reference requirements; list original-asset production needs and distinct permission/provenance records. No sprite recolor or generic primitive may be counted as the final full-3D model.
13. Create a theatre/terrain/props register and terrain-mechanic matrix from shipped maps/assets. Record which bridges/ice/tunnels/water/cliffs/buildability/destructibles actually exist and which rules are still unverified. Route bridge/world features to RES-025 with exact map/build evidence.
14. Create a source-to-Fusion disposition for every entity and mechanic: faithful profile, authored adaptation proposal, campaign/scenario-only, reference-only, verified inactive alias/exclusion, or unresolved. Unsupported engine capabilities block affected entities; do not fake them with a generic unit type.
15. Reconcile candidate counts to final categories and compare manual roster, active definitions, producer menus and campaign/map references. Explain every discrepancy, alias and exclusion. Submit complete era package for independent review and emit the downstream per-entity implementation draft only from verified included records.

## Acceptance and negative checks

- Every named faction/class/country/house in this card has a complete positive-and-negative availability graph or explicit evidence blocker; no group silently inherits all units from another faction.
- Every discovered raw candidate has a stable classification and disposition. The report shows discovery, verified shipped, included, inherited alias, inactive, unresolved and excluded counts separately; it never calls raw file counts playable totals.
- All included entities have cited specification fields and source-behavior fixtures. Signature mechanics listed in the era matrix have evidence and capability IDs; unavailable capability implementations remain blocked rather than cosmetically approximated.
- The era delta against its parent, campaign/civilian/neutral content, transform/deploy/summon relationships and terrain/bridge cases are reconciled. No valid released object is silently dropped to simplify implementation.
- Content completeness remains uncertified while critical data, permission/provenance or active-build contradictions are unresolved. Independent research reviewer signs the package and records sampled high-risk fixtures.
- Every factual claim used by downstream implementation is traceable to a recorded source or repeatable observation; uncertainty is represented explicitly with owner and resolution action.
- The artefacts are machine-parseable where specified, use stable IDs, contain no unresolved placeholder text disguised as final data, and have an independent reviewer assigned.
- A clean handoff lists changed files, exact reference versions, unresolved blockers, dependent capability IDs, next tasks, and the checks actually performed. A blocked critical datum prevents its affected implementation from being released, without blocking unrelated research.
