## Procedure

1. Assign immutable requirement IDs to owner requirements, source-derived mechanics, terrain and profile-preservation constraints.
2. Link each to its source/decision, task, test/visual criterion and exact candidate identity.
3. On a schema or rule change calculate downstream task/assets/test impact and reopen only affected evidence.
4. Require expected/actual raw results and reviewer identity; distinguish partial, blocked and failed criteria.
5. Validate the ledger at every integration candidate and reject missing references, removed mandatory rows or evidence from incompatible versions.
6. Publish concise progress by verified requirements and remaining blockers, not output volume or token use.

## Required deliverables

- requirements/evidence schema and validator
- versioned traceability ledger
- change-impact and gate readiness reports

## Acceptance and negative checks

- Each sentence claiming completion resolves to accepted candidate-specific evidence.
- Changing a pack/rule/schema invalidates the correct dependent evidence rather than all unrelated progress.
- Mandatory failed/unverified rows prevent the relevant gate from closing.

## Guardrails

- Refresh exact source and candidate revisions before asserting implementation or deployment status.
- A missing source, rights decision, skipped check or placeholder script remains explicitly unverified.
- Use existing authorization; do not invent repeated approval requests for reversible work.

## Handoff

Return the exact revision, changed records/files, criterion-by-criterion expected/actual/evidence/verdict, independent reviewer, remaining blockers and next ready task.

The handoff must include exact base/head and manifest hashes; changed paths; each criterion with expected/actual result and evidence; commands and exit codes; reviewer identity; blockers and next action. A mandatory failed, blocked or unverified criterion cannot be converted to Done by an unresolved-disposition note.
