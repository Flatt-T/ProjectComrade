## Procedure

1. Pin the first-battle seed to verified RA1 Allied/Soviet reference profiles from RES-005 plus explicitly cited bridge observations. Enumerate exact source rows before accepting downstream values; this manifest is the bounded denominator, not an invented final roster. Required seed coverage: MCV deployment, power, sidebar production, ore gathering/unloading, infantry/Engineer, tracked vehicles and turrets, aircraft/rearm, naval/submerged movement, targeting/armour/projectiles, capture/repair, faction support powers and authored bridge damage/rebuild.
2. Read completed source packages needed by the pinned seed and record other era packages as extensions. Accept only the pinned seed scope here; emit bounded per-era extension work and unresolved source rows for RES-028/029 whole-series closure.
3. Normalize construction policies: MCV/yard/sidebar/ground-control, mobile/deploy production, dozer/worker build sites, faction-specific RA3 placement/cores/tech clearance and C&C4 crawler/class production. Preserve cancellation/refund and ownership lifecycle distinctions.
4. Normalize economies: field harvesting/regrowth/storage, ore nodes, supplies and collectors, secondary income, salvage/recycling, support-power resources, upgrade resources and command-point caps/refunds. Separate world/meta economy from tactical resources.
5. Normalize locomotion/navigation: foot/wheel/track/hover, amphibious/naval/submerged, fixed-wing/VTOL/docking, jump/jetpack, subterranean, tunnel transport, teleport and transform states; annotate layers, collision, turning, acceleration, path/blocking and occupancy constraints.
6. Normalize combat/interactions: target domains, armor/warheads, projectile/beam/arcs, wind-up/reload/rearm, squads/hardpoints, deploy abilities, capture/infiltration/repair, garrison clearing, passengers, mind control capacity/overload/release, parasitic/attached effects, cloning/spawn children, wreck salvage and stealth/detection.
7. Normalize powers/environment: EMP, shield/invulnerability, radiation/toxin/fire/ice, chrono deletion/displacement, freeze/shrink/magnetism, weather/ion effects, nuclear/ion/particle/psychic support, superweapon charging, terrain destruction and bridge lifecycle.
8. For every capability in the pinned seed define the minimal engine contract: input commands, state variables, timing, authoritative ownership, movement/target/build effects, lifecycle on death/capture/transform/save-load, deterministic replay/network requirements and observable acceptance. Map existing runtime support as real, partial, absent or unknown based on repository audit.
9. Record conflicting evidence by profile and patch. Prefer active-build reproduction for actual behavior, preserve code/manual facts separately, and make any Fusion resolution a new decision rather than deleting historical differences.
10. Publish capability dependency graphs and per-entity blockedBy references. Require the engine architect and independent historian to approve each new module boundary before task generator creates dependent content implementation leaves.
11. Produce an extension manifest for every remaining era/faction/capability/theatre/feel/adaptation row, with responsible source/PKG owner, required evidence and bounded follow-up work. Global reconciliation belongs to RES-028/029; no absent era is silently omitted or marked complete here.

## Acceptance and negative checks

- Every signature mechanic in the pinned seed has a capability ID, evidence, lifecycle and fixture.
- No absent crawler, transformation, mind-control, subterranean or bridge-layer behavior is marked supported by a generic stats-only unit.
- Cross-era conflicts remain explicit profile differences and authored Fusion decisions, with source values retained.
- Engine owners can identify exactly which entity leaves are blocked by a capability and what fixture unlocks them.
- Every factual claim used by downstream implementation is traceable to a recorded source or repeatable observation; uncertainty is represented explicitly with owner and resolution action.
- The artefacts are machine-parseable where specified, use stable IDs, contain no unresolved placeholder text disguised as final data, and have an independent reviewer assigned.
- A clean handoff lists changed files, exact reference versions, unresolved blockers, dependent capability IDs, next tasks, and the checks actually performed. A blocked critical datum prevents its affected implementation from being released, without blocking unrelated research.
- The seed manifest is explicit, source-pinned and independently reviewed; remaining whole-series obligations have bounded extension owners and are checked by RES-028/029 rather than hidden in an unfinished prerequisite.

## Guardrails

- Treat this card as a bounded contract. Read the named context and dependency outputs first. If required evidence is missing, record a precise blocked datum and continue only with independent work; never fill a field with an assumed zero, a plausible remembered value, or another era's value.
- Preserve native Classic fidelity independently from source-era research profiles and the newly authored Fusion rules. A Fusion change is an explicit adaptation decision with rationale and acceptance tests, never a retroactive claim about the historical game.
- Cite each nontrivial historical statement or numeric datum to an exact manual page, immutable source path/line or symbol, or timestamped observation in an identified shipped build. Search snippets, fan wikis, source declarations and official announcement text cannot alone prove shipped behavior.
- Keep code licence, trademarks, audiovisual assets, music, voice, map files and gameplay research as separate provenance questions. Do not redistribute retail game files or copied art/audio in the repository; original homage assets or specifically permitted material need their own provenance record.

- Do not change GitHub, Linear, live game configuration, billing, or other external services under this research card. Commit only the approved research deliverables in an isolated branch; the fleet supervisor owns integration and publication.
- Write factual conclusions, calculations, test setup, results and concise decision rationale. No private reasoning trace is required. Spend available analysis on the assigned bounded task; do not inflate output or exceed the context window merely to claim exhaustive thought.
